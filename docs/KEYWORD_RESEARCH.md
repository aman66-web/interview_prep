## Result against the playbook's gate

The gate for a November ad test (Day 21) is at least 5 relevant terms, not counting the three firm names, with a low top-of-page bid of £0.60 or less, **and** 10 or more reviewed cases live.

- Terms at £0.60 or less: UK 1 ("consulting case interview", £0.51); US 0 (the lowest is £0.62).
- **The first half of the gate is not met**, so the playbook's answer is: no ad spend in November; give the time to societies, search pages and Reddit.
- Search volume is also small: the biggest practice terms are about 20 to 40 UK searches a month and 200 to 400 US searches a month, so even cheap clicks would give very few visitors. "market sizing questions" (140 UK, 320 US) and "case interview" (110 UK, 880 US) are the largest, which suits search pages more than ads.
- Revisit only if the Day 21 review wants to try it anyway (the founder's choice), or after launch when real search-term data exists.
