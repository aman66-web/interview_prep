### Data processing agreements (part of each provider's standard terms)

- Supabase: Terms of Service, section 7(b): "The Parties agree to comply with the Data Processing Addendum, which is incorporated into this Agreement" (no date shown on the page). `https://supabase.com/terms`. The Data Processing Addendum (Version 1, 1 August 2026) "supplements and forms part of the Supabase Terms of Service" and says acceptance of the agreement "shall have the same effect as signing the SCCs". `https://supabase.com/legal/dpa` (the terms link to the same text at `https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/data-processing-addendum`).
- Vercel: Terms of Service (last updated 1 June 2026), section 10.1: personal information is processed "in accordance with Vercel's Data Processing Addendum ("DPA"), which is incorporated by reference". `https://vercel.com/legal/terms`. The Data Processing Addendum (last updated 17 March 2026, effective 31 March 2026) "shall become legally binding upon Customer entering into the Agreement". `https://vercel.com/legal/dpa`.
- Anthropic: Commercial Terms of Service (effective 17 June 2025): data "will be processed in accordance with the Anthropic Data Processing Addendum ... which is incorporated into these Terms by reference". `https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms`. The Data Processing Addendum (effective 24 February 2025) "is incorporated into and forms part of the Anthropic Commercial Terms of Service". `https://www.anthropic.com/legal/data-processing-addendum`.
- Stripe: Data Processing Agreement (last updated 28 September 2026) "is subject to and incorporated by reference into the Agreement", meaning the Stripe Services Agreement. `https://stripe.com/legal/dpa`. It also says Stripe acts as a controller for some purposes, such as fraud prevention and complying with the law (the basis for the policy's line on Stripe's own use of payment data).
- Vercel's DPA applies as a processor agreement only for customers "on Enterprise and Pro plans" (DPA section 1; section 4(a)). The project must stay in the founder's Pro team: on Hobby there would be no processor DPA and the policy's DPA sentence would stop being true. Section 4(b): "To the extent that any Service-Generated Data is considered Personal Data and as to any Contact Data, Vercel is the Controller"; "Service-Generated Data" is "usage data and metadata that is generated through the use of the Services", and its data subjects include end users (Schedule 1). Terms section 10.4 lets Vercel use "System Data" ("traffic data, telemetry, logs") for its own purposes. This is the basis for the policy's line on Vercel's own use of usage data. Lawyer to check whether visitors' IP addresses in request logs count as Customer Data (Schedule 1 section 5 lists "IP address") or Service-Generated Data.

### Transfer safeguards

- Supabase: the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914) and, for the UK, the ICO's "Approved Addendum" (version B.1.0). The DPA does not mention the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. `https://supabase.com/legal/dpa`. The ICO's own document is titled "International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, VERSION B1.0" (`https://ico.org.uk/media2/migrated/4019539/international-data-transfer-addendum.pdf`), which is why the policy calls it the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.
- Vercel: Schedule 3 of the DPA: "For data transfers from the United Kingdom, the UK IDTA will be deemed entered into ... together with the Standard Contractual Clauses" (the 2021 SCCs). Vercel calls Schedule 5 the "UK IDTA", but its text is the ICO's Addendum B1.0 to the SCCs ("the template Addendum B.1.0 issued by the ICO and laid before Parliament ... on 2 February 2022"), not the separate ICO International Data Transfer Agreement. So the policy's name, "UK International Data Transfer Addendum", is right; never write "International Data Transfer Agreement" for Vercel. No Data Privacy Framework mention. `https://vercel.com/legal/dpa`.
- Anthropic: Schedule 3 of the DPA: the EU SCCs (Modules Two and Three) and, for UK GDPR data, the ICO's Approved Addendum (version B.1.0). No Data Privacy Framework mention. `https://www.anthropic.com/legal/data-processing-addendum`.
- Stripe: Data Transfers Addendum (last updated 18 November 2025): one mechanism only, in this order: "(a) the Data Privacy Framework; (b) the EEA SCCs or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum"; UK data sent to Stripe in the US is received by Stripe, LLC "under the Data Privacy Framework". The Addendum itself never says "UK Extension"; that name comes from Stripe's privacy policy and DPF policy. `https://stripe.com/legal/dta`. Stripe's Privacy Policy (last updated 28 April 2026) says Stripe, LLC complies with the EU-U.S. DPF, "the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. DPF" and the Swiss-U.S. DPF. `https://stripe.com/privacy`.
