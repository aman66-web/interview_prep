### Transfer safeguards

- Supabase: the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914) and, for the UK, the ICO's "Approved Addendum" (version B.1.0). The DPA does not mention the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. `https://supabase.com/legal/dpa`. Vercel calls Schedule 5 the "UK IDTA", but its text is the ICO's Addendum B1.0 to the SCCs ("the template Addendum B.1.0 issued by the ICO and laid before Parliament ... on 2 February 2022"), not the separate ICO International Data Transfer Agreement. No Data Privacy Framework mention. `https://vercel.com/legal/dpa`.
- Anthropic: Schedule 3 of the DPA: the EU SCCs (Modules Two and Three) and, for UK GDPR data, the ICO's Approved Addendum (version B.1.0). No Data Privacy Framework mention. `https://www.anthropic.com/legal/data-processing-addendum`.
