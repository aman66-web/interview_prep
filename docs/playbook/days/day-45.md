**Text to use (post, email or message)** (copy exactly):

````text
Practising alone works best for the parts you can drill. A routine that fits round other things:

1. Maths, 10 minutes a day, without a calculator. Percentages, growth rates and big multiplications with lots of zeros. Say the units out loud.
2. Structure. Take any business question ('should a gym chain open in a new city?'), give yourself 60 seconds, then say your structure out loud. Check it covers the market, competitors, whether the company can win, and the money.
3. Charts. Take any chart from a company report and say in one sentence what it shows before you look at the detail.
4. Full cases. Read a case one question at a time and answer each out loud before you read on. Record yourself once a week and listen back.
5. Then get some live practice with a person (a society case club, friends, alumni), because the back-and-forth is the hardest part to copy alone.
````
