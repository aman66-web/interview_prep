````text
Revenue is down 10% and customer numbers are down 5%. How much did spend per customer fall?

Not 5%. Revenue = customers × spend per customer, so spend per customer = 0.90 ÷ 0.95 = about 0.947, a fall of about 5.3%.

For small changes, adding the percentages gets you close. For big ones it doesn't: if customers fall 30% and spend per customer falls 30%, revenue falls 51%, not 60% (0.7 × 0.7 = 0.49).

In a case, write the multiplication down, then decide whether 'about 5%' is close enough to say out loud.

````
