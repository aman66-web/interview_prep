**Text to use (post, email or message)** (copy exactly):

````text
Margin and markup aren't the same thing, and mixing them up is an easy slip in a case.

Say a product costs £80 and sells for £100:
- Markup is profit ÷ cost: £20 ÷ £80 = 25%.
- Margin is profit ÷ price: £20 ÷ £100 = 20%.

So a 25% markup is a 20% margin. When an interviewer says 'margin', put the price underneath, not the cost.

A quick check when you're unsure: for a profitable product, the margin is always the smaller of the two.

````
