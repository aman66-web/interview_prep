A simple routine for case maths, 10 to 15 minutes a day:

1. Percentages: build them from 10% and 5% (15% of 80 is 8 + 4).
2. Big numbers with units: for 3.2 million × 45, do 3.2 × 45, then add the 'million'.
3. Growth: 'up 10%, then down 10%' ends 1% lower. Make sure you can say why.
4. One chart or table a day: say the so-what in one sentence before any numbers.

Do it by hand. McKinsey and Bain both say calculators aren't allowed in their interviews (check their careers pages).
