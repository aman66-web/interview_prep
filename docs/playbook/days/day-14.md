````text
Three things to do before you read a single number in a case exhibit:

1. Read the title out loud. It tells you what the chart is for.
2. Check the units. Thousands or millions? Pounds or dollars? Per month or per year?
3. Find the one thing that stands out: the biggest bar, the line that turns, the row that doesn't fit.

Then say the so-what in one sentence, for example: 'Revenue grew in every region, but the North gave half of the growth, so I'd like to understand what's different there.'

That last sentence is the part interviewers listen for.
````
