Three mental maths shortcuts that come up all the time in cases:

1. Times 25: multiply by 100, then divide by 4. 36 × 25 = 3,600 ÷ 4 = 900.
2. Divide by 5: double it, then divide by 10. 340 ÷ 5 = 680 ÷ 10 = 68.
3. Divide by 25: multiply by 4, then divide by 100. 1,250 ÷ 25 = 5,000 ÷ 100 = 50.
