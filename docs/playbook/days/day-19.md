**Text to use (post, email or message)** (copy exactly):

````text
Big numbers trip people up in market sizing. One habit fixes most of it: split each number into a small number and a count of zeros.

3 million × 40 thousand:
- 3 million is 3 with 6 zeros. 40 thousand is 4 with 4 zeros.
- Multiply the small numbers: 3 × 4 = 12.
- Add the zeros: 6 + 4 = 10.
- 12 with 10 zeros is 120 billion.

Dividing works the other way round: 60 million ÷ 2 thousand is (60 ÷ 2) with (6 − 3) zeros, so 30 with 3 zeros: 30,000.

Say the units out loud at every step and you'll catch most slips before the interviewer does.

````
