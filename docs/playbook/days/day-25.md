````text
Quick one. A price goes up 10%, then down 10%. Where does it end up?

Not where it started. It ends 1% lower: £100 becomes £110, then £99.

The second change is 10% of a bigger number. The same thing catches people with growth: a business that falls 50% has to grow 100% to get back to where it was.

In a case it matters when a client says 'we lost 20% of our customers last year and grew customer numbers by 20% this year'. They're still 4% down: 100 × 0.8 × 1.2 = 96.

Free drills on percentage change and growth:
````
