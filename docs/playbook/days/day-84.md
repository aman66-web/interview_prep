One habit that makes a case easier to follow: a 15-second summary each time you finish a part.

'So far: costs are flat, and the fall in profit is all on the revenue side, mainly fewer customers in the North. Next I'd like to understand why those customers left.'

It does three things:
- It shows the interviewer you know where you are.
- It catches mistakes early, yours or theirs.
- It gives you a clean start for the next part.

Try it in your next practice case: summarise every time you switch branches. It feels slow at first. It isn't.
