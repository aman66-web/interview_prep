# Cover note for the case reviewer

Thank you for reviewing CaseSpar's practice cases. This pack holds one file per case, each with the answer key: what the candidate is told, the facts and exhibits the interviewer gives, the maths with working, and the model answer outline.

## Batch 1 (please start here, in this order)

1. `harbourline-bakeries.md`: the free starter case, so the most important one (profitability, easiest level)
2. `wrenfield-hotels-profit.md` (profitability, easy)
3. `us-dog-walking-market.md` (market sizing, easy)
4. `hollinsway-pet-insurance.md` (market entry, easy)
5. `flexora-gym-software-pricing.md` (pricing, medium)

The other cases are in the `later` folder. Please leave them until batch 1 is done.

## What to do for each case

Answer the six questions at the end of its file (realistic, clear, fair, the answer, difficulty and time, verdict), then finish with exactly one of:

- Sign off as it is
- Sign off after these small changes (say what)
- Needs rework (say why)

The background notes on each case and the open questions for you are in `docs/CASE_REVIEW_NOTES.md`; the full brief is in `docs/REVIEWER_GUIDE.md`.

## Two rules

- Please do not copy or adapt cases from casebooks, firms or other prep sites into your suggestions. Every case must stay original.
- Please keep the cases and answer keys confidential: don't share them or use them anywhere else.

## Notes

- All companies and numbers are invented. The maths is checked by automated tests, but please tell us if any figure looks unrealistic for the industry.
- A case takes about 20 to 30 minutes to review.
